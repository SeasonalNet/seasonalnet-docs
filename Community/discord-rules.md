# Official SeasonalNet Communications Server Rules

**Effective date:** June 30, 2026
**Last updated:** June 30, 2026

SeasonalNet began as SCPT Community PBX, evolved into SeasonalPBX, and now operates as SeasonalNet. This server is the official community, support, and project-discussion space for SeasonalNet and its services.

By participating in this server, you agree to follow these rules. Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply independently. Access to SeasonalNet-operated services is also governed by the current SeasonalNet Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.

Discord separately processes activity on its platform under its own terms and privacy policies.

## 0. Compliance with Discord requirements

Members must comply with Discord’s Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and other applicable platform requirements.

Discord requires users to meet the minimum age applicable to their country or region. A person who does not meet that requirement must leave this server and stop using Discord.

If SeasonalNet staff or administrators become aware that an underage person has accessed the server or provided personal information to SeasonalNet, SeasonalNet may:

* Remove the person from the server.
* Take appropriate steps to remove their personal information from SeasonalNet-controlled systems, as required by applicable law.
* Report the account or circumstances to Discord when appropriate.
* Take reasonable steps to prevent the person from regaining access until they meet the applicable minimum-age requirement.

SeasonalNet does not control information retained or processed independently by Discord.

## 1. Respect other members

Treat other members with respect and address disagreements constructively.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discriminatory slurs, sexual harassment, targeted humiliation, encouragement of self-harm, or sustained hostile conduct are prohibited.

Criticize ideas and technical decisions, not the person presenting them.

## 2. Use channels appropriately

Keep discussions in the most relevant channel and follow any channel-specific guidance.

Do not repeatedly cross-post the same material, derail active discussions, or create duplicate support requests.

When requesting technical help:

* Provide relevant context and symptoms.
* Describe what you have already attempted.
* Use code blocks or attachments where appropriate.
* Remove credentials, access tokens, private keys, personal information, and other secrets from logs or screenshots.

## 3. Do not spam or misuse promotion

Do not flood channels, abuse bot commands, post repetitive or meaningless content, or send unsolicited promotional direct messages to members.

Referral links, advertisements, unrelated self-promotion, and invitations to other communities require prior staff approval.

Links to relevant projects, documentation, or services may be shared when they directly contribute to a legitimate discussion.

## 4. Use mentions responsibly

Mention only users or roles that are relevant to the request, and do so sparingly.

Repeated pings, mass pings, attention pings, or attempts to pressure staff members or volunteers into responding are prohibited.

A role or individual should not be mentioned repeatedly merely because a response has not yet been received.

## 5. Do not post malicious or dangerous material

Do not post, distribute, or solicit:

* Malware or destructive payloads
* Phishing or credential-stealing content
* Stolen credentials, tokens, certificates, or private keys
* Fraudulent instructions or deceptive services
* Material intended to compromise another person, account, device, service, or network
* Instructions whose primary purpose is unauthorized access, disruption, surveillance, or abuse

Defensive security discussion is permitted when conducted lawfully and responsibly.

Active scanning, exploitation, vulnerability testing, or similar testing of SeasonalNet systems requires explicit prior authorization under the SeasonalNet Acceptable Use Policy.

## 6. Do not distribute unlawful, stolen, or pirated material

Do not use the server to distribute, request, or facilitate:

* Pirated software or media
* Cracked or stolen license keys
* Stolen accounts or data
* Fraud or theft services
* Unlawful surveillance
* Material that violates another person’s rights
* Other unlawful activity

Technical discussion of a subject does not authorize members to use the server to carry out or facilitate prohibited activity.

## 7. Protect privacy and confidential information

Do not disclose another person’s private information without authorization.

This includes real-world identity details, addresses, telephone numbers, private messages, support tickets, account records, credentials, or non-public infrastructure information.

Do not repost private conversations or staff communications without the consent of the people involved, except when submitting relevant material privately as evidence in a report or appeal.

SeasonalNet staff may review server messages and retain reports, tickets, moderation records, and related evidence where reasonably necessary to operate and protect the community.

## 8. Maintain account and identity integrity

Do not impersonate another member, staff member, service, project, organization, or network.

Do not use alternate accounts, deceptive identities, compromised accounts, or other methods to evade moderation, restrictions, or previous enforcement actions.

Members are responsible for maintaining control of their Discord accounts and should promptly report suspected account compromise.

## 9. Keep content appropriate for the community

Pornographic or explicit sexual content, sexual content involving minors, graphic gore, shock content, and other material unsuitable for a general technical community are prohibited.

Staff may remove content that is materially disruptive, unsafe, or clearly inconsistent with the server’s purpose even when the exact conduct is not exhaustively listed in these rules.

## 10. Treat support as best-effort

SeasonalNet staff and community members provide assistance voluntarily and without a service-level guarantee.

Do not demand immediate responses, repeatedly reopen answered requests, abuse people who decline or cannot provide support, or misrepresent community assistance as a guaranteed service obligation.

This server is not an emergency service. It must not be treated as the sole source of emergency, safety, weather, telephony, medical, legal, financial, or other time-critical information.

## 11. Report violations without escalating them publicly

Use the designated support, ticket, or reporting process to report suspected violations.

Do not:

* Start public confrontations over reported conduct
* Retaliate against a reporter
* Encourage harassment or pile-ons
* Continue an argument after staff have directed it to stop
* Present yourself as a moderator when you are not one

Preserve relevant evidence and allow staff to review the matter.

Good-faith reports will not result in enforcement merely because staff ultimately determine that no violation occurred.

## 12. Moderation and enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, staff may:

* Remove or restrict content
* Issue a formal or informal warning
* Restrict access to roles, channels, threads, or server features
* Apply a timeout
* Remove a member from the server
* Ban an account
* Preserve relevant evidence or moderation records
* Refer misuse of SeasonalNet services for separate action under the SeasonalNet Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy

Enforcement may be immediate where reasonably necessary to protect members, systems, services, or the community. Progressive discipline is not guaranteed.

Staff may consider the severity of the conduct, apparent intent, surrounding context, prior history, cooperation, risk of recurrence, and effect on other members.

Moderation discretion does not eliminate the appeal process described below.

## 13. Appeals

A member may request review through the designated support channel or ticket process.

An appeal must be concise, truthful, and non-abusive. It should identify the action being appealed and explain why the member believes it should be changed.

A different available staff member may review the action when practical. SeasonalNet does not guarantee that every appeal will result in a different decision.

Public arguments, repeated appeals without new information, harassment of staff, retaliation, or attempts to evade the action may result in further enforcement.

## 14. Changes to these rules

These rules may be updated when SeasonalNet, its services, the community, or relevant risks materially change.

The effective and last-updated dates identify the current version. SeasonalNet will announce material changes in the server when they take effect.

Continued participation after an updated version takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised rules.
